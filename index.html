Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services

What services do you offer at Johnz Empire?

We offer comprehensive digital solutions including ICT Training & Consultancy, Digital Marketing & SEO, Graphic & Brand Design, Blogging & Content Creation, Software Installation & IT Support, and Web & Mobile App Development.

How does your consulting process work?

We follow an 8-step consulting process: 1) Discovery & Needs Assessment, 2) Strategy Consultation, 3) Proposal & Agreement, 4) Design & Development, 5) Testing & Quality Assurance, 6) Client Review & Approval, 7) Training & Handover, and 8) Ongoing Support & Maintenance.

Do you provide training for individuals and organizations?

Yes, we offer ICT training for individuals, institutions, and corporate teams. Our training covers ICT basics, web design & development, blogging & online income, graphics design, online business mentorship, and ICT for schools and organizations.

What is your typical project timeline?

Project timelines vary based on complexity. Simple websites take 2-3 weeks, while complex applications may take 2-3 months. We provide detailed timelines in our project proposals and maintain regular communication throughout the process.

Do you offer ongoing support after project completion?

Absolutely! We provide ongoing support and maintenance packages that include technical support, system updates, and service maintenance plans to ensure your solution continues to perform optimally.

How can I get started with a project?

You can start by contacting us through our contact form, WhatsApp, or email. We'll schedule a free consultation to discuss your needs and provide a customized proposal.
